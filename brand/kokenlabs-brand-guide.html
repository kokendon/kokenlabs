Brand essence

From chaos to clarity.

Koken Labs puts AI into production for companies that are done talking about it. The look is edgy. The work underneath is rigorous. Both have to be true on every piece.

Belongs to

The Koken family

Koken Labs is the AI build arm of the Koken family. Its job in the family is to be the most technical, most modern-looking member.

Feels

Edgy, rigorous

Dark ground, one bright gradient signal, monospace labels. It should feel like a lab console, not an agency deck.

Promises

Shipped, in weeks

Every visual and every line backs up one claim: working software in production, fast, at a clear price.

Logo

The Koken Labs wordmark

Use the supplied files. Never retype the logo in a font, and never rebuild the ō by hand.

k / kenSpace Grotesk SemiBold (600), lowercase, −2 tracking
The ōAn open ring under a bar, in the Nova gradient. It replaces the o and is the one element that never changes color in the full-color logo.
LABSJetBrains Mono Bold (700), caps, +2 tracking, Nova Violet, sitting just above the baseline
SpacingThe ō sits 9 units off each neighbor. Don't tighten or open it up.

Use the full-color logo whenever the medium can show the gradient. Save the one-color versions for places that can't: embroidery, engraving, single-ink print, fax-quality documents and some partner logo walls.

Logo use

Space, size and the ō mark

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the width of the ō (shown as x). Nothing else goes inside that zone: no text, no edges, no other logos.

x

Minimum size

LABS is the first thing to break when the logo gets small. Below these sizes, switch to the ō mark.

Sizes are a starting rule set for v1.0. Check them on a real print proof before a large run.

The ō mark

The ō on its own. Use it for avatars, favicons, app icons, slide corners and anywhere the full name won't fit. On the app icon it sits on an Obsidian rounded square.

Don't

Also avoid: putting the full-color logo on the Nova gradient (the ō disappears), dropping or retyping LABS, setting the wordmark in capitals, swapping the ō for a plain o, and placing the logo over busy photos without an Obsidian panel behind it.

Typography

Three faces, three jobs

All three are free on Google Fonts. Space Grotesk speaks, Inter explains, JetBrains Mono labels. Don't let one do another's job.

Display & headings
Space Grotesk

SemiBold 600. Headlines, buttons, numbers, the wordmark.

Body
Inter

Regular 400, Medium 500. Paragraphs, nav, captions.

Labels & data
JetBrains Mono

Medium 500, Bold 700. Eyebrows, tags, status lines, LABS.

Write the name as two words, both capitalized: Koken Labs. Not KokenLabs, Koken labs or KOKEN LABS. The lowercase "koken" with the ō appears only inside the logo. The tagline is From chaos to clarity., with the period.

Outline the logo text before sharing the files

The current SVGs set "k", "ken" and "LABS" as live text that loads Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts. That works on the website. In email, Canva, slide decks and at print shops the fonts often don't load, and the logo falls back to a default font. Convert the text to outlines once and ship those files as the masters.
